# Content Opportunities — gauraconstruction.lk

**Date:** 3 September 2026

The site already publishes 17 competent guides. The gap is not *volume* — it is **first-hand evidence**. Almost every existing guide is advice that a competent writer could produce without ever having built anything in Sri Lanka. What Gaura uniquely holds, and currently does not publish, is:

- Real project cost, duration and scope data across 10+ delivered projects
- Actual approval timelines from named local authorities
- Named, credentialed in-house engineers who can author with authority
- The cost-tier dataset already powering the calculator

**No opportunity below requires inventing anything.** Each is a documented instruction to publish data the company already holds. Where the data does not exist internally, that is stated as a blocker.

## Priority 1 — Turn existing project data into original research

### O1. Sri Lanka Construction Cost Report 2026 (flagship)

| | |
|---|---|

| **URL** | `/construction-cost-report-sri-lanka-2026` |

This is the single highest-leverage asset available. Cost-per-square-foot is the most-searched construction question in Sri Lanka, and a sourced dataset with a named RICS-accredited author is genuinely citable — by journalists, by competitors, and by answer engines. It converts an existing tool into a research publication.

**Required limitation to state plainly:** rates derive from Gaura's own completed projects, so they reflect one contractor's cost base, not a national average. Saying so *increases* citation-worthiness.

## Priority 4 — District differentiation

The 25 district pages are ~1,900 words each but differ mainly by place name, neighbouring towns and province. They are not a penalty risk, but they cannot rank strongly on templated text.

**Recommended approach — depth before breadth:**

**Tier 1 (real delivered projects — differentiate first):** Colombo, Gampaha, Kurunegala, Rathnapura, Hambantota
Add: the actual project(s) delivered there, the local authority process, district-specific cost variance, site conditions (coastal salt exposure, hill-country access, high water table).

**Tier 2 (service coverage, no projects yet):** the remaining 20
Leave as coverage pages. **Do not** manufacture local detail. When a project completes in a district, promote it to Tier 1 with real content.

This is the honest structure: pages differentiate as real evidence accrues, not before.

## Explicitly not recommended

| Idea | Why not |
|---|---|
| Adding `Review`/`aggregateRating` for on-site testimonials | Outside Google's review-snippet policy for first-party reviews, however genuine. Use Google Business Profile. |
| Sub-district or city pages (Nugegoda, Maharagama, …) | Would be near-duplicates of pages that are already templated. Doorway-page territory. |
| Publishing "average Sri Lankan construction cost" as a national figure | Gaura's data reflects one contractor's cost base. Stating the limitation is what makes O1 credible. |
| High-frequency AI-generated posting | The existing 17 guides are competent but generic. The gap is evidence, not volume. |
| Naming clients or values without consent | Several projects are commercial (AMR Traders, Trinity, CEYPETCO, Nipuna Stores). Get written consent first. |
