Why you need a land survey before you build

  • Confirms your land's real boundary and extent before design and construction start.
  • Needed by the local council - Pradeshiya Sabha, Urban Council, Municipal Council, or UDA - to approve your building plan and permit.
  • Usually required by the bank if you're applying for a construction loan or mortgage.
  • Helps avoid boundary disputes with neighbours and rejected permit applications later.

When people usually need a land survey

  • Buying a block of land, or checking a title before you commit to a purchase.
  • Getting ready to build a new house, shop, or commercial building.
  • Dividing a family land parcel between siblings, or for sale.
  • Applying for a construction loan that requires a current survey plan.
  • Sorting out an old, unclear, or disputed deed before building or selling.

How a land survey works with Gaura Construction

  1. Site visit and document review. We look at your deed, any existing survey plans, and the land itself to work out what's needed.
  2. Licensed surveyor field survey. A Survey Department-licensed surveyor measures the boundary, extent, or subdivision on site.
  3. Survey Department processing. The plan is prepared and taken through the Survey Department's approval steps.
  4. Handover and integration. You get the approved plan, and we build it into your house plan, permit application, or property paperwork.

Frequently asked questions about land surveys

  • How much does a land survey cost in Sri Lanka? Cost depends on the land's extent, location, and the type of survey needed - a simple boundary check generally costs less than a subdivision or condominium plan. Send us your land details and we'll come back with a quote.
  • How do I get a land survey done in Sri Lanka? Gaura Construction arranges the process for you: we review your deed and site, bring in a Survey Department-licensed surveyor to measure the land, and take the plan through Survey Department approval before handing it over.
  • How long does a land survey take? Timelines vary with land extent, site access and Survey Department workload. A straightforward boundary survey is generally quicker than a subdivision or condominium plan, which involves extra approval stages.
  • Do I need a land survey before building a house? Yes. Local authorities need an approved survey plan to process your building permit, and it protects you from boundary disputes with neighbours once construction starts.
  • What is the difference between a land survey and a soil test? A land survey measures your land's boundary, extent and access. A soil test checks the ground's bearing capacity for foundation design. Most construction projects need both before design starts.
  • Where can I find a licensed surveyor near me in Sri Lanka? Gaura Construction coordinates Survey Department-licensed surveyors for projects across Colombo, Gampaha, Kandy, Galle, Kurunegala and other districts. Contact us with your land's location and we'll arrange a surveyor.
  • What is a Survey Department approved survey plan? It's a survey plan prepared by a licensed surveyor and processed through the Survey Department of Sri Lanka. It becomes the official record of your land's boundary and extent, and is needed for deeds, subdivisions, building permits and property sales.
  • Is a land survey plan required for a bank loan? Yes. Sri Lankan banks typically require a current approved survey plan along with the deed as part of the documentation for construction loan and mortgage applications.
